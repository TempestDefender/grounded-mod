## 🧩 About

**Grounded Mod** is a comprehensive mod menu for Grounded featuring overlay, trainer, resource multiplier, no hunger, unlimited durability, speed boost, teleport, and item spawner.

Based on popular mods like **Grounded Mod Menu**, **Grounded Trainer**, and **Grounded Scripts**.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Mods
- Overlay — in-game menu with toggle hotkey
- Resource Multiplier — multiply gathered resources (1x–100x)
- No Hunger — disable hunger and thirst
- Unlimited Durability — tools and weapons never break
- Speed Boost — adjust movement speed (1x–5x)

### 🎨 Cosmetics & Unlocks
- Unlock All Items — access all crafting recipes and gear
- Teleport — fast travel to any location
- Item Spawner — spawn any item by ID

### 🏋️ Training & Utility
- God Mode — take no damage from creatures
- Infinite Breath — swim without drowning
- Build Anywhere — place structures without restrictions
- Save/Load Positions — quick teleport points
